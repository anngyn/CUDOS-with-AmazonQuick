Verify that Amazon QuickSight is active in your account and has permissions to access S3 billing buckets and Athena databases.

## Step 1 — Verify QuickSight Permissions

1. Open **Amazon QuickSight → Admin Settings → Security & Permissions**.
2. Ensure **Amazon S3** and **Amazon Athena** are checked under AWS service access.
